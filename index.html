Song warm-ups

One song a day, about 3 minutes, no coach needed. Each routine is built from the song’s key, chords and range: find home, the scale, the chords, a range stretch and the song’s style. Every step: listen, then your turn, graded. Then film your reel.

All 20 songs

    Voice care: warm-ups should feel easy. If anything scratches or feels tight, sing softer, skip that step, sip water. Strong, never painful.

    Hollow head = long (half or whole). Filled head = short (quarter or less). Each flag or beam halves it. A dot adds half again. The time signature says how many beats fill a bar: 3/4 = 3 beats per bar, so a dotted half fills a whole bar. In 6/8 count eighth notes: 6 per bar.

    Sight-singing

    A new short melody every time. Read the syllables, hear only the starting note, then sing it and get graded. Levels build from steps to leaps to chromatic notes.

    Interval ear training

    Two notes play. Which distance was it? Each answer shows the solfège that makes that interval, so the sound gets linked to syllables you already sing.

    Solfège basics

    Solfège gives every note of a key a syllable. With "movable do", do is always the home note of the song’s key, so the same syllables work in every key.
